Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still curious about something? Here are the questions we hear most often.

What is the Upfinity Digital X-Ray, and is it really free?

Yes, completely free. Submit your website and/or social accounts, and we'll send back a short report covering three things you're doing well and three clear opportunities — no obligation to work with us afterward.

How is pricing determined for my project?

The pricing cards on this page are sample packages to give you a sense of scale. Every real quote is customised — we scope the work around your goals, current setup, and budget before we finalise a number.

What's included in your AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)?

Website updates, security and uptime monitoring, hosting support, and small content changes throughout the year — at some of the lowest AMC rates in the industry.

How often will we meet to discuss progress?

Every client gets a standing monthly meeting where we walk through results in plain language and agree on priorities for the month ahead. Growth and Enterprise plans get more frequent check-ins.

How long does a typical website project take?

A standard 5–10 page website usually takes 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how much content and feedback we're waiting on from your side.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Absolutely — most of our collaboration happens over calls and email, so location isn't a barrier. We're happy to work with businesses anywhere.

UPFINITY — NETLIFY CONTACT FORM

Contact form code:
- contact.html contains a static Netlify form named "contact".
- The form includes data-netlify="true" and the hidden form-name field.
- The service checkboxes are inside the form.
- js/contact.js submits URL-encoded data to Netlify's standard "/" endpoint.

DEPLOYMENT:
1. Publish the contents of this folder as the site publish directory.
   index.html and contact.html must be at the publish-directory root.
2. Trigger a fresh production deploy in Netlify.
3. Open Netlify > Forms and confirm that a form named "contact" appears.
4. Only after it appears, test the live form.

If "contact" still does not appear, check the Netlify site's Build & deploy
settings: the configured publish directory must point to this folder (the
folder containing index.html and contact.html). Form detection happens during
deployment; JavaScript cannot register a form by itself.
